Nepal Medical Care - Prescription Refill Requests

HOW TO REQUEST A REFILL / HOW DO I GET A PRESCRIPTION REFILL
There are three ways to request a refill:
- Call the clinic and ask ARIA, the AI assistant, to route a refill request.
- Ask your pharmacy to send a refill request to the clinic.
- Submit a refill request through the patient portal.
A refill request by phone requires identity verification (full name and date of birth).

WHAT THE ASSISTANT NEEDS FOR A REFILL REQUEST
- The name of the medication (name only).
- Your preferred pharmacy name and location.
The assistant confirms the medication name and pharmacy, then routes the request to your
provider. The AI assistant does not approve, deny, change a dose, or discuss whether a
refill is appropriate - every request is reviewed by the prescribing provider.

HOW LONG A REFILL TAKES / REFILL PROCESSING TIME
Routine refill requests are reviewed and processed within 48 to 72 business hours after
the request is received. Requests submitted on a Friday or before a holiday may take
until the next business day to begin processing. Please send refill requests at least 5
days before you run out of medication.

URGENT REFILLS
If you have less than 2 days of medication left, say so when you call. The request is
flagged as urgent and prioritized, but an urgent refill may still require a same-day or
next-day appointment before the provider will approve it.

CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES
Controlled substances cannot be refilled by phone or through the AI assistant. These
include opioid pain medications, ADHD stimulant medications, certain anti-anxiety
medications, and some sleep medications. A refill for a controlled substance requires an
office visit, and these prescriptions are generally reviewed every 3 months. Early
refills are not provided.

WHEN A VISIT IS REQUIRED BEFORE A REFILL
A refill may be held until you are seen if:
- You have not had an office visit in the past 12 months.
- The medication requires lab monitoring that is overdue.
- You are reporting side effects or asking for a dose change.
- The prescription has no refills remaining and the provider wants to reassess.

AFTER YOU REQUEST A REFILL
The assistant tells you the request has been sent to your provider for review. You will
be contacted, or the pharmacy will be notified, once the provider makes a decision. If
you have not heard back after 72 business hours, call the clinic to check on the status.

PHARMACY INFORMATION
The clinic can send prescriptions electronically to any pharmacy in the United States.
Have your pharmacy's name and city ready when you call.
