Nepal Medical Care - Emergency Guidance

IF THIS IS A LIFE-THREATENING EMERGENCY / WHEN TO CALL 911
If you or someone with you has a life-threatening emergency, hang up and call 911
immediately, or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not wait for a call back and do not
use the clinic phone system for emergencies. Nepal Medical Care is an outpatient clinic
and does not provide emergency care.

WHAT COUNTS AS AN EMERGENCY
Call 911 right away for any of the following:
- Chest pain, chest pressure, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw.
- Trouble breathing or shortness of breath.
- Signs of a stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred or confused speech, sudden
  severe headache.
- Severe or uncontrolled bleeding.
- Fainting, collapse, or unresponsiveness.
- A severe allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, difficulty swallowing,
  widespread hives with breathing trouble.
- Thoughts of harming yourself or someone else. You can also call or text 988, the
  Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, at any time.
- Severe burns, a major injury, or a broken bone with visible deformity.
- Poisoning or a suspected overdose. You can also call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.

HOW THE AI ASSISTANT HANDLES EMERGENCY LANGUAGE
If a caller mentions emergency or acute symptom language, the AI assistant stops all
scheduling activity immediately, tells the caller to hang up and call 911 if it is
life-threatening, and transfers the call to the clinic's emergency line without asking
any further questions. This behavior overrides every other step in a call.

URGENT BUT NOT LIFE-THREATENING
For urgent concerns that are not emergencies - for example, a fever that will not come
down, a minor cut that may need stitches, a possible sprain, or a medication reaction
that is uncomfortable but not severe - call the nurse line at (650) 555-0111 during
clinic hours. The nurse can advise you and, if needed, arrange a same-day visit.

AFTER CLINIC HOURS
The clinic is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Outside these hours:
- For an emergency, call 911.
- For urgent medical questions, call the nurse line at (650) 555-0111 and follow the
  after-hours instructions to reach the on-call clinician.
- For non-urgent needs, call back during clinic hours or use the patient portal.

WHAT THE AI ASSISTANT WILL NOT DO
The AI assistant does not give medical advice, assess symptoms, diagnose, or decide
whether a situation is an emergency. If you are unsure how serious a situation is, treat
it as an emergency and call 911.
