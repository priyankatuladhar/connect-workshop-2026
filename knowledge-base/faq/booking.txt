Nepal Medical Care - Booking an Appointment

HOW TO BOOK AN APPOINTMENT
Appointments can be booked three ways:
- By phone through ARIA, the AI assistant, during clinic hours.
- By speaking with a front-desk team member.
- Through the patient portal at any time.
When booking by phone, the caller must first pass identity verification (full name and
date of birth). New callers can have an account created on the same call.

WHAT INFORMATION IS NEEDED TO BOOK
To book an appointment the assistant collects:
- The provider you want to see, or the type of care you need.
- A preferred date and time, or a general window such as "morning" or "afternoon".
- The visit category: follow-up, annual physical, new patient visit, lab work, or
  specialist referral.
The assistant records only the visit category, never a description of symptoms. Before
the booking is finalized, the assistant reads back the provider, date, time, and reason
and waits for the caller to say yes. After booking, a confirmation code is given and an
SMS confirmation is sent.

SAME-DAY APPOINTMENTS / CAN I BOOK A SAME-DAY APPOINTMENT
Same-day appointments are available for urgent but non-emergency needs, subject to
provider availability. Call as early in the day as possible; same-day slots are limited
and usually fill by late morning. Same-day requests are more likely to be accommodated
with a General Practice provider than with a specialist. If no same-day slot is open,
the assistant will offer the earliest available alternatives.

WALK-IN POLICY / CAN I WALK IN WITHOUT AN APPOINTMENT
Walk-in patients are seen Monday through Friday before 4:00 PM, subject to provider
availability. Walk-ins are worked in between scheduled appointments, so wait times vary
and can be long. Booking ahead by phone or portal is always recommended. Walk-ins are
not accepted in the last two hours before closing so that scheduled patients are seen on
time. Specialist visits (Cardiology, Dermatology) are by appointment only and are not
available as walk-ins.

NEW PATIENT APPOINTMENTS
New patient visits are longer than routine visits. Please plan for up to 60 minutes and
arrive 15 minutes early to complete intake. Bring a photo ID, insurance card, a list of
current medications, and records from any previous provider if available.

REFERRALS
Cardiology and Dermatology appointments generally require a referral from a primary care
provider. If you do not have a referral, book with a General Practice or Internal
Medicine provider first, or ask the front desk whether your plan requires one.

BOOKING WINDOW AND CONFIRMATION
Appointments can be booked up to 90 days in advance. You will receive an SMS confirmation
after booking and a reminder message the day before your visit. Keep the confirmation
code - it is used to look up, reschedule, or cancel the appointment.

ARRIVING FOR YOUR APPOINTMENT
Arrive 15 minutes before the scheduled time. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late,
the visit may need to be rescheduled so that other patients are seen on time.
