Nepal Medical Care - Rescheduling and Cancellation

NOTICE PERIOD / HOW MUCH NOTICE DO I NEED TO GIVE
To reschedule or cancel an appointment without a fee, give at least 24 hours notice
before the scheduled start time. Changes made with less than 24 hours notice, and
missed appointments with no notice, are treated as late cancellations.

HOW TO RESCHEDULE AN APPOINTMENT
Rescheduling can be done by phone through ARIA, the AI assistant, by a front-desk team
member, or through the patient portal. By phone, the caller must pass identity
verification first. The assistant looks up the existing appointment, so the caller does
not need to know the confirmation code. The assistant states the current date and time
and the proposed new date and time, and waits for the caller to say yes before making
the change. A new confirmation code and SMS confirmation are sent after rescheduling.

HOW TO CANCEL AN APPOINTMENT
Cancellation can be done by phone, by a front-desk team member, or through the patient
portal. By phone, the caller must pass identity verification. The assistant reads back
the appointment being cancelled and waits for an explicit yes before cancelling. A
cancellation confirmation is sent by SMS.

LATE CANCELLATION AND NO-SHOW FEE / IS THERE A FEE
A $25 fee applies to:
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment.
- No-shows (missing an appointment without cancelling).
The fee is $40 for new patient visits and specialist appointments because those slots
are longer and harder to fill. Same-day cancellations due to sudden illness are excused -
call as soon as you can and let the team know.

REPEATED MISSED APPOINTMENTS
Three no-shows within a 12-month period may result in a review of the patient's standing
with the clinic and, in some cases, discharge from the practice. The front desk contacts
the patient before any such decision.

RUNNING LATE
If you are running late, call the clinic. Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may be
asked to reschedule so that other patients are seen on time. This counts as a same-day
change, not a no-show, and no fee applies if you called ahead.

CLINIC-INITIATED CHANGES
If the clinic must reschedule your appointment - for example, a provider is out sick -
the front desk calls you directly to arrange a new time. No fee ever applies to a change
the clinic initiates.

CONFIRMATION CODES
Every appointment has a confirmation code sent by SMS at booking. You do not need the
code to reschedule or cancel by phone, because the assistant looks up your appointments
after verifying your identity, but keeping the code helps if you use the portal.
