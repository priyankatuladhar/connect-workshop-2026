Nepal Medical Care - General Clinic Information

ABOUT THE CLINIC
Nepal Medical Care (NMC) is a primary care and specialty outpatient clinic. We provide
administrative front-desk services through ARIA, our AI phone assistant: new patient
registration, appointment booking, rescheduling, cancellation, appointment lookup,
insurance verification, and prescription refill routing. Clinical advice, diagnosis, and
triage are never provided over the phone.

CLINIC HOURS / OPENING HOURS / WHEN DO YOU OPEN
The clinic is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
The clinic is closed on Saturday and Sunday.
The clinic is closed on major public holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day,
Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.
On weekdays the clinic opens at 9:00 AM and closes at 6:00 PM. The last appointment of
the day is scheduled at 5:30 PM. Phone lines and the AI assistant are available during
these same hours.

PARKING AND TRANSIT
Limited on-site parking is available for a few cars and a two-wheeler bay; paid street
parking is available along Lazimpat Marg. The clinic is a two-minute walk from Lazimpat
Chowk and is served by local buses and micros toward Maharajgunj and Ratna Park.

VISIT TYPES WE OFFER
The clinic schedules these visit categories: follow-up visits, annual physical exams,
new patient visits, lab work, and specialist referrals. When you call, ARIA only records
the visit category - never a description of symptoms.

SERVICES NOT OFFERED AT THIS CLINIC
We do not provide emergency care, surgery, advanced imaging (MRI or CT), physical
therapy, mental health counseling, or dental and vision care. For these, your provider
will refer you to a partner facility.

INSURANCE / DO YOU ACCEPT MY INSURANCE
Nepal Medical Care accepts most major PPO and HMO plans, Medicare Part B, and California
Medicaid (Medi-Cal). Commonly accepted plans include Aetna, Blue Shield of California,
Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Kaiser (out-of-network only), and Anthem. Coverage varies by
individual plan. To confirm whether a specific plan is accepted, ask ARIA to check the
plan by name, or call the billing team at (650) 555-0100. There is no payment collection
through the phone system; billing questions go to the front desk.

LANGUAGE AND ACCESSIBILITY
Interpreter services, including Nepali, Hindi, and Spanish, are available at no cost.
Please tell the front desk when scheduling if you need an interpreter. The facility is
wheelchair accessible and offers accessible exam rooms.
