Nepal Medical Care - Identity Verification

WHY IDENTITY VERIFICATION IS REQUIRED
Nepal Medical Care must confirm who a caller is before sharing any protected health
information (PHI) or making changes to an appointment. This protects patient privacy and
meets HIPAA requirements. Caller ID alone is never enough - phone numbers can be shared
by a family or spoofed - so every caller completes verification even when the system
already recognizes their number.

WHAT YOU NEED TO VERIFY YOUR IDENTITY / HOW DO I VERIFY WHO I AM
To be verified, a caller must provide two matching items:
1. Full legal name as it appears on the patient record.
2. Date of birth.
If both match an existing patient record, the caller is verified for the remainder of
that call.

WHEN VERIFICATION HAPPENS
Verification is required before any of the following:
- Disclosing appointment details (date, time, provider).
- Disclosing insurance or account information.
- Booking, rescheduling, or cancelling an appointment.
- Routing a prescription refill request.
General questions - clinic hours, location, directions, policies, how to prepare for a
visit - do not require verification and can be answered for anyone.

IF VERIFICATION FAILS
If the name and date of birth do not match, the caller is asked to repeat both items
once. After two failed attempts, the call is transferred to a front-desk team member who
can help confirm identity through additional questions. The AI assistant will not
continue with a protected action until verification succeeds.

CALLING ON BEHALF OF SOMEONE ELSE / FAMILY MEMBERS
The AI assistant can only act for the verified patient on the line. It cannot look up or
change information for a spouse, parent, adult child, or any other person, even a family
member, unless that person is the verified caller. A patient who wants someone else to
manage their care must have a signed authorization on file with the front desk. Parents
or legal guardians may act for their own minor children (under 18) after verifying the
child's name and date of birth.

NEW CALLERS WITH NO RECORD
If the phone number and details do not match any patient, the caller is treated as new.
The AI assistant can offer to create a new patient account, which requires full name,
date of birth, and a contact phone number. After the account is created, the caller
still completes identity verification before any protected action.

WHAT WE NEVER ASK FOR OVER THE PHONE
The AI assistant never asks for a Social Security number, full credit or debit card
number, or online portal password to verify identity. Verification uses only name and
date of birth.
